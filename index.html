Engineering Objective & Problem Statement

The ArogyaNidan Edge-360 is engineered to solve the critical bottlenecks of rural healthcare delivery in India: a 68% shortage of specialists at Community Health Centres (CHCs), lack of cold-chain infrastructure for diagnostic reagents, and an 18% Inverted GST structure that penalizes local manufacturing. By integrating a 4-wavelength micro-spectrophotometer, digital piezo-acoustic stethoscope, polarized macro-camera, and zero-internet edge AI into a single IP67 drop-resistant handheld, it is intended to support Auxiliary Nurse Midwives (ANMs) with preliminary screening and triage at community level; clinical performance and intended-use claims remain subject to validation.

Cold-Chain Free Microfluidics

Accepts single-drop capillary blood (40 µL) onto cyclic olefin copolymer (COC) dry-reagent discs. Microfluidic channels deliver quantitative results for Hb, HbA1c, Glucose, Creatinine, Electrolytes, and Bilirubin without liquid reagent refrigeration.

On-Device Edge-AI Engine

Powered by a dual-core 32-bit RISC-V microcontroller with an integrated 1.2 TOPS Neural Processing Unit (NPU). Executes INT8 quantized neural models for conjunctival anemia screening, retinal vascular scoring, and pulmonary sound classification without cellular internet.

Microfluidic Architecture & Spectrophotometry Physics

The core innovation of the ArogyaNidan Edge-360 is its ambient-stable microfluidic disc architecture. Utilizing capillary action and lyophilized dry reagents, the disc isolates serum and measures absorbance across 4 distinct optical wavelengths.

12-Parameter Microfluidic Disc Specification

Injection-molded Cyclic Olefin Copolymer (COC) disc with laser-ablated capillary valves

Sample Input Volume: 40 µL capillary whole blood (single fingerprick)
Shelf Life Stability: 24-month / 45°C stability design target; accelerated and real-time stability studies pending
On-Disc Plasma Separation: Integrated membrane filter separates red cells in 12s

Conjunctival Anemia & Retinopathy

Design Objective: Sensitivity ≥92.4% | Specificity ≥89.1% — to be established by prospective validation
Acoustic AI Model

Acoustic Spectrogram Classifier

1D-CNN model (1.8 MB size) processing digital lung sound spectrograms to distinguish wheezes, crackles, and bronchial breath sounds associated with Pneumonia & COPD.

Design Objective: Sensitivity ≥94.1% | Specificity ≥91.5% — to be established against an appropriate clinical reference standard
Metabolic AI Model

CKD & NCD Risk Predictor

XGBoost decision tree fused into C-code (850 KB size). Combines blood creatinine, glucose, BP, and age to output 5-year Chronic Kidney Disease risk tier.

Design Objective: AUC-ROC ≥0.93 for defined renal-risk endpoint — prospective validation and endpoint definition required

Design for Manufacturing & Regulatory Compliance Dossier

To bypass the 18% Input vs 5% Output Inverted GST Structure, the ArogyaNidan Edge-360 is engineered for assembly using 85% domestically sourced polymers and micro-controllers within the Andhra Pradesh MedTech Zone (AMTZ).

5-Step Domestic Assembly Protocol

1
Injection Molding Chassis Assembly Local PC/ABS housing molded at AMTZ plastic fabrication line.
2
SMT PCB Surface Mounting 4-layer RISC-V motherboard populated via high-speed SMT line in Visakhapatnam.
3
Optical Engine Calibration LED wavelength/output calibration to traceable standards is planned; AMTZ/NIST traceability is a proposed verification route, not a completed test.
4
IP67 Waterproof Sealing Ultrasonic welding of rubber gaskets and port covers.
5
Burn-In & QC Testing 48-hour thermal cycling is a planned QC gate; acceptance criteria and verification records must be established before release.

Regulatory & Verification Roadmap

Gate 1: Engineering Verification Months 1 - 6

Electrical safety, EMC/EMI, environmental, usability and analytical verification against the final device configuration.

Gate 2: Clinical / Performance Validation Months 7 - 12

Prospective analytical and clinical-performance evaluation against appropriate reference methods, with predefined endpoints and statistical analysis.

Gate 3: Classification & Licensing After evidence package

Final classification and applicable licence pathway to be determined under the Medical Devices Rules, 2017 after intended-use freeze. ICMED may be pursued separately as a quality-certification pathway.

Current Regulatory Basis (India)

Risk classification

Medical devices and IVDs are risk-classified A–D under the Medical Devices Rules, 2017. Final classification depends on intended use and applicable classification provisions; it should be confirmed for the final configuration.
